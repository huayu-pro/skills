03 / Studio

Design for
the long now.

We are a small team of architects, makers and researchers. We work slowly where it matters: material, proportion, and the way a room changes at four in the afternoon.

Our practice moves between built work, spatial research and limited objects. Every project is developed in close conversation with the people who will use it.
